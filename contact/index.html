General and support enquiries

For questions about the app, bug reports, account problems, or billing:

support@shieldnav.com

We aim to respond within two business days. If you are reporting a problem in the app, please include your device model, iOS version, and a description of what happened — it saves a round trip.

Our Support page already answers the most common questions, including how to cancel a subscription, request a refund, or delete your account.

Privacy and data requests

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data — including requests under the CCPA:

support@shieldnav.com with the subject line “Privacy Request”

We process verified requests within 30 days. See our Privacy Policy for the full description of your rights and what data we hold.

Crime data corrections

If a record on the map looks wrong, tell us. Email support@shieldnav.com with the subject “Data Issue”, including the city, the location, and what appears incorrect. In-app, use Settings → Development Feedback → Data Issue.

Because our records come from municipal open data, some corrections have to be made at the source by the publishing agency. We will tell you when that is the case.
