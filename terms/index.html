3. Description of the Service

3.1 Features of the Service

ShieldNav provides GPS-assisted, crime-aware navigation. ShieldNav is offered as a single subscription product; there is no free tier and no separate feature tiers. Subject to an active subscription (including an active free trial), the Service includes:

  • Turn-by-turn voice navigation powered by the HERE Maps SDK;

3.2 Fair Use, One Active Drive, and the Demo

Each subscription is for one person. To keep it that way and to protect the paid services behind the app, the following limits apply:

  • One active drive at a time. Each account or guest session can navigate on one device at a time. If you start a drive on a second device while the first is still navigating, the second device is refused new routes and reroutes and shows a message saying so. A drive that is already under way is never cut off: the first device keeps its directions. If the first device stops checking in for about three minutes (for example, because it lost signal or the drive ended without closing the app), another device can take over.
  • Rate limits. Our servers limit how quickly any one account or device can request routes and searches (for example, about 30 route requests per five minutes). Normal use never reaches these limits; they exist to stop automated or shared use.
  • The demo. Before subscribing you can try a limited demo with a small daily allowance of routes and searches. Demo sessions end after 24 hours.

Requests refused under these limits are recorded, with your account ID and install identifier, so we can detect misuse, as described in our Privacy Policy. Repeated attempts to get around these limits, or to share one subscription among several people, are a violation of these Terms.
