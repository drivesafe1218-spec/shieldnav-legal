What’s in ShieldNav

Navigation, crime data, and what a subscription adds

Navigation

ShieldNav is a complete turn-by-turn navigation app, not a crime map with directions attached. Routing, guidance, and search are powered by the HERE Maps SDK, a mapping platform used widely in built-in automotive navigation systems.

  • Spoken turn-by-turn guidance with automatic rerouting when you leave the planned route.
  • Route planning between any origin and destination, with the crime layer visible on the same map.
  • Point-of-interest search for finding destinations by name or category.

All of the above is available to every registered user without a subscription.

The crime layer

ShieldNav assembles crime data from open records published by city and county governments, then normalizes each jurisdiction’s format into a common structure so a single map can span multiple cities.

Free accounts see a heatmap overlay showing reported incident density in an area. A Premium subscription opens the full crime radar, which surfaces the individual records behind the heatmap — incident type, date, and block-level location.

Important limits

Crime data is historical and depends entirely on what each jurisdiction publishes and how often. Coverage varies by city, refresh cadence varies by feed, and reported incidents are not a complete record of what occurred. ShieldNav does not predict crime, score neighborhoods, or make claims about the people in an area. Please read the Crime Data Disclaimer in full — it covers accuracy limits and our Fair Housing position.

Saved places and scheduled drives

Save home, work, and any custom destination so they are one tap away, and schedule drives ahead of time. Saved places, recent destinations, and scheduled drives are stored against your account rather than only on the device, so they survive a phone upgrade.

You can view, correct, or delete any of this from within the app. See the Privacy Policy for what is stored and for how long.

Accounts and sign-in

ShieldNav requires a registered account so your saved data can follow you across devices. You can sign in with:

  • Email and password, with email verification and password reset;
  • Sign in with Apple, including Apple’s private email relay if you prefer not to share your address;
  • Google Sign-In.

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Account → Delete Account, or by emailing us. See Support for the full procedure.

Requirements and availability

  • Platform: iPhone. An Android version is not currently in development.
  • Permissions: location access is required for navigation and for the crime layer to centre on where you are.
  • Connection: an internet connection is required for routing, search, and crime data.
  • Status: in active development, not yet released on the App Store. Join the waitlist to hear at launch.

Technology and data sources

We name our providers rather than describing them vaguely, because you should be able to check what your data touches.

  • Mapping and routingHERE Maps SDK — navigation, places search, speed limits, toll estimates
  • Crime recordsOpen data published by municipal and county governments
  • Accounts and storageFirebase (Google LLC) — authentication and account data
  • SubscriptionsRevenueCat — subscription status; Apple processes payment
  • Sign-in providersSign in with Apple, Google Sign-In

What each of these receives, and what they do not, is set out in section 6 of our Privacy Policy.
