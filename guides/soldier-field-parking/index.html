Soldier Field Parking: Lots, Shuttles and Game Day Planning for Bears Fans

By The ShieldNav Team · Last checked November 21, 2026

Heading in from the suburbs for a Bears game or a Museum Campus event? Here is how the lots work, which ones need a pass, where the free shuttles run, and where rideshare pickup is.

Quick answers

  • Lots next to the stadium require a prepaid pass and open four hours before kickoff.
  • No pass? Park at a drive-up garage or lot and take a shuttle.
  • Rideshare: pick-up and drop-off at Balbo and Columbus Drives, north of the stadium.
  • Accessible parking: all lots are ADA compliant.

Official lots and prepaid parking

Single-game passes for limited Soldier Field lots are sold at SoldierFieldParking.com. Passes are sent electronically; print them or scan from your phone.

Cash is not accepted at the coupon-lot entrances on Bears days.

Drive-up garages and lots

  • Millennium Garage and Millennium Lakeside Garage (Columbus Dr. between Monroe and Randolph): no tailgating, free GEICO shuttle.
  • Grant Park North and South: regular posted daily rate, no shuttle.
  • McCormick Place Lot B (31st St.): shuttle provided, tailgating allowed; cash spaces may be limited on large-show days.
  • McCormick Place Lakeside Garage, Lot C: no tailgating, no shuttle.
  • 18th Street Lot and Adler Planetarium Lot: tailgating allowed.
  • Burnham Harbor Lot: available beginning in November.

Prices: the Bears and Soldier Field pages state event rates vary and are subject to change, with no standard rates or dates listed.

Street parking rules

No parking is allowed in the residential area bounded by Roosevelt Road (north), Cermak Road (south), DuSable Lake Shore Drive (east) and Clark Street (west). Violators face ticketing and towing. Overnight parking is not permitted at Museum Campus lots.

Getting there and traffic

  • From I-55: north to Lake Shore Drive, exit at 18th Drive.
  • From I-290 or I-94 (Kennedy): Congress Parkway to Columbus Drive, then south to 18th Drive.
  • From I-90/94 (Dan Ryan): north to the Lake Shore Drive/22nd Street exit, stay right onto Lake Shore Drive north, exit at 18th Drive.
  • Lots close three hours after day games, or right after night games, and may close earlier.
  • Tailgating ends two hours after the game.

Shuttles, transit and rideshare

  • GEICO Gameday Shuttle (free): runs between the Millennium Garages and the stadium, starting three hours before kickoff until two hours after the final whistle. Post-game pick-up is at the 18th Street turnaround.
  • Lot B shuttle: starts two hours before kickoff, runs until two hours after the game.
  • CTA: Red, Orange or Green Line to Roosevelt, then bus 146 or walk east on Roosevelt Road.
  • Metra: #128 Soldier Field Express from Ogilvie (board on Madison) or Union Station (board on Jackson east of Canal); South Shore Line to McCormick Place, then walk one block north.
  • Rideshare and taxi: Balbo and Columbus Drives. Buses may only use Columbus Drive north of Balbo; buses, taxis and limos may not enter campus from 90 minutes after kickoff until one hour after the game.

Accessible parking

All lots are ADA compliant. Lots next to the stadium require a prepaid coupon. Without one, park at Lot B or a Millennium Garage and take the free ADA-accessible shuttle to the ADA turnaround at Gate 14.

After the game

  • Lots may close early, so know your lot’s closing time.
  • Post-game shuttle pick-up is at the 18th Street turnaround.
  • Also heading to the West Side? See the United Center lots and prepaid options or the Wrigleyville game-day parking guide.

FAQ

Do I need a prepaid pass?

For lots immediately around the stadium, yes. Drive-up options exist farther out.

Is there a free shuttle?

Yes, the GEICO Gameday Shuttle from the Millennium Garages, plus a Lot B shuttle.

Can I tailgate?

In designated lots only; open alcohol is not allowed outside the lots or on shuttles.

Where do taxis and rideshare pick up?

Balbo and Columbus Drives, north of the stadium.

Can I park in the nearby residential area?

No; ticketing and towing apply.
