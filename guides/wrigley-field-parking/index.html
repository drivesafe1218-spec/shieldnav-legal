Wrigley Field Parking: A Game Day Guide for Cubs Games and Concerts

By The ShieldNav Team · Last checked November 21, 2026

Driving into the North Side for a Cubs game or concert? Street parking is tightly controlled around the ballpark, so most drivers choose a lot, a remote shuttle lot, or transit. Here is how each works.

Quick answers

  • All Cubs lots are cashless.
  • Free remote lot for night and weekend games: 4650 N. Clarendon Ave., with a free shuttle (new location in 2026).
  • Night games: the nearby residential permit zone is a tow zone from 5 p.m.
  • Rideshare pick-up: Addison (Broadway to Halsted) or Irving Park Road (Clark to Seminary).

Chicago Public Schools lots are open for Cubs games: Hawthorne Scholastic Academy (3338 N. Seminary Ave.), Inter-American Magnet School (808 W. Addison St. garage; 3639 N. Fremont Ave. lot) and Walt Disney Magnet School (4105 and 4151 N. Clarendon Ave.). Night games open at 5 p.m., day games at 10 a.m., and lots close one hour after the game ends.

Prepaid parking

Parking can be reserved in advance through SpotHero, per the Cubs; availability changes. ParkWhiz and similar sites also list nearby private lots.

Street parking rules

  • Around the ballpark, Residential Permit Parking Zone 383 (Belmont to Irving Park, Broadway to Ashland) is a tow zone from 5 p.m. to 10 p.m. on night game days. Enforcement starts at 5 p.m.
  • Vehicles blocking bus stops, crosswalks, driveways or alleys can be ticketed and towed immediately.
  • Commercial trucks are barred from the area bounded by Halsted, Racine, Belmont and Irving Park from three hours before to one hour after an event.
  • Guest passes apply only to residents’ guests. Reselling permits can bring fines up to $1,500.
  • Cubs neighbor hotline: 1-866-427-3869 (one hour before to two hours after games and concerts).

Getting there and traffic

  • Choose your lot or remote lot before you leave and arrive early; nearby streets are the most restricted.
  • The Cubs encourage transit for events.

Transit and rideshare

  • CTA Red Line, Addison station, is the direct, accessible stop.
  • Buses: 152 Addison (extra service from two hours before to one hour after games), 22 Clark, and 8 Halsted.
  • Howard Park & Ride: $5, 550+ spaces, then ride the Red Line.
  • Pace Wrigley Field Express serves weekend home games and weekend concerts (pacebus.com/wrigleyfield).
  • Bike valet: free, in the alley just east of the Red Line entrance on Addison; opens three hours before the game.
  • Rideshare pick-up zones: Addison from Broadway to Halsted, and Irving Park Road from Clark to Seminary.

Accessible parking

Ask the Cubs Fan Services team about ADA parking: 773-388-8270. The Addison Red Line station is accessible.

After the game

  • Lots close one hour after the game; remote shuttles run about 90 minutes.
  • Rideshare pick-up is in the zones above, not on the side streets.

FAQ

Is there free parking for Cubs games?

The Cubs list a free remote lot at 4650 N. Clarendon Ave. for night and weekend games, with a free shuttle.

Can I pay cash?

No, all Cubs lots are cashless.

Can I park on the street?

Check posted signs; Zone 383 is a tow zone from 5 p.m. on night game days.

Where do rideshare pickups happen?

Addison (Broadway to Halsted) and Irving Park Road (Clark to Seminary).

Is there a bike option?

Yes, a free bike valet.
