United Center Parking: A Practical Guide for Bulls, Blackhawks and Concert Nights

By The ShieldNav Team · Last checked November 21, 2026

Driving to the United Center from the suburbs? This guide covers the lots, how to prepay, where rideshare drops off, and what changed for the 2026-27 season, so you can plan the drive before you leave.

Quick answers

  • Drive-up or prepaid? Both exist. Some lots are drive-up; others are coupon-only. Buying ahead is generally the cheaper route.
  • Where do rideshares pick up? The Uber Zone is at Madison and Wood (Lot E).
  • Accessible parking? Lots C and H have ADA spaces for vehicles with ADA placards.
  • Lots open: one hour before the doors open.

United Center official lots

For 2026-27 the arena lists its lots by type:

Construction matters this season. The first phase of the 1901 Project began in June 2026 on the former Lot K and Lot G, taking more than 1,100 spots offline. The arena added several previously private lots a few blocks east and west to make up the difference.

Prepaid parking and prices

  • Parking can be bought on arrival (first-come, first-served) or in advance through Ticketmaster.
  • The arena’s recommended booking partner is SpotHero; ParkWhiz is another booking option.
  • Lot Pass holders use the ALT Lot Pass (W, M, R, T, P, E) or the PRFRD Lot Pass (A, B, C, F, MXC).
  • Price signal: as of October 2026, the Chicago Sun-Times reported drive-up rates rising by as much as $15 to $30 at some lots, with advance coupons starting at $20. Not every closer-in lot offers advance coupons. Rates change by event, so confirm at checkout.
  • Some drive-up lots were converted to prepaid-only at the start of the season, and prepaid lots can fill early. Keep a second lot in mind.

Garages and other lots nearby

Private garages and lots in the surrounding blocks sell spaces through booking sites. Terms, hours and exit times vary by operator, so read the listing before you pay.

Getting there and traffic

  • Weeknight Blackhawks games now start at 7 p.m., 30 minutes earlier than before, which compresses arrival times.
  • The Oct. 6 home opener saw prepaid lots fill and heavy post-game traffic, according to the Sun-Times; fans reported a smoother experience at later games.
  • Plan to arrive early, and decide on a lot before you leave home.

Transit and rideshare

  • CTA 19 United Center Express runs for Bulls and Blackhawks games and selected events. After events, buses run every few minutes for up to one hour.
  • Rideshare: the Uber Zone at Madison and Wood opens two hours before games and stays open up to one hour after. The arena also describes a designated pick-up and drop-off zone steps from the entrances.

Accessible parking

Lots C and H have ADA spaces for vehicles with ADA placards. Call or check the arena’s page before the event.

After the game

  • Expect heavy traffic as lots empty at once. Waiting a short while before leaving is a common approach.
  • Rideshare pick-up stays open up to one hour after the game.
  • Before you head out, set your route to your car or home and review the route options.

FAQ

Can I buy parking at the gate?

Yes at drive-up lots, first-come, first-served. Prepaying is generally cheaper.

Do I need a coupon for every lot?

No. Lots A, B, C, F and MXC are coupon-only; W, M, L, R, T, P and E are drive-up.

What time do lots open?

One hour before the gates.

Is there a ride from downtown by bus?

The CTA 19 United Center Express serves Bulls and Blackhawks games and selected events.
