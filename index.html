Where the crime data comes from

Crime data from CrimeoMeter, which collects the incident reports that police departments and city governments publish on their open-data portals. It currently covers Chicago, Schaumburg, Downers Grove, and Hanover Park, Illinois.

  • Coverage varies by city. A place that does not publish open incident data has no crime layer. The map shows what was reported and published, not everything that happened.
  • The data is historical, not live. Departments publish on their own schedules, some daily and some with a lag of weeks. ShieldNav shows the most recent data available.
  • It describes places, not people. It covers reported incidents in an area. It is not a prediction, not a judgment about the people who live or work there, and not a substitute for your own awareness while driving.

Common questions

Why not just use my regular map app?

Your map app shows traffic. It doesn’t show the crime reported along the way. ShieldNav is full turn-by-turn navigation with three routes every trip, each with the crime reported along it, so you can pick the Safest route before you leave. It has its own navigation and doesn’t work inside other map apps.

What does ShieldNav show me?

Turn-by-turn driving directions, with incidents reported to police shown on the same map. You can see reported incident activity along a route or around a destination before you set off.

Which cities have crime data?

Chicago, Schaumburg, Downers Grove, and Hanover Park, Illinois. Navigation works anywhere HERE has maps, but the crime layer only appears where we have data.

Is it free?

New subscribers get a 7-day free trial with everything included. After that it is one auto-renewing subscription, billed through the App Store or Google Play, and you can cancel any time. No account is needed to subscribe.

Is there an Android version?

Yes. ShieldNav is on Google Play for Android phones (Android 8.0 or later) and on the App Store for iPhone.
