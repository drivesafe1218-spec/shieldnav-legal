6 things your free map app isn’t telling you before you drive

Your map app is great at one thing: getting you there fast. But every Chicagoland driver knows the fastest drive isn’t always the one you want. Here’s what it leaves out, and how ShieldNav fills the gaps.

The six things

  1. 01

    It only knows one word: fastest.

    Your app picks the quickest route and stops there. If you’d rather take the route with less reported crime, you’re on your own to figure it out.

    ShieldNav shows your route options before you leave, labeled Safest and Fastest, each with its ETA, traffic delay and the crime reported along it. Then you choose.

  2. 02

    It knows the traffic on a street, not what crime was reported there.

    Your map can tell you a road is backed up. It can’t tell you what crime incidents were reported on it last week.

    In ShieldNav, tap any incident to see what was reported, when, and how close it was to your route. A vague worry becomes a plain fact.

  3. 03

    It stops caring once you arrive.

    The drive is only half of it. For most of us, the parking spot and the walk in are the part we think about.

    ShieldNav’s Parking Safety checks the crime reported around your drop-off before you get there, so you know what to expect before you pull in.

  4. 04

    Once you’re driving, you’re on your own.

    One detour or one wrong turn can put you on a block you didn’t plan on.

    ShieldNav is full turn-by-turn navigation, and Crime Radar keeps you aware of incidents reported nearby while you drive, not just before you leave.

  5. 05

    It’s paid for by ads.

    Most free map apps make their money from advertising, and your drives help pay for it.

    ShieldNav has no ads. Ever. We don’t share your data with ad networks. It’s a subscription after a free 7-day trial, so the app answers to you, not advertisers.

  6. 06

    Where you drive is nobody’s business but yours.

    Where you go, when you go and where you park says a lot about you.

    ShieldNav never sells your data. No data brokers. We don’t keep a history of where you drive, there’s no location tracking when the app is closed, and you can use it without an account, even as a subscriber.
